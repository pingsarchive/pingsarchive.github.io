The composition reflects Diaz’s broader interest in using the appearance of scientific diagrams to explore subjects that move between the measurable and metaphysical. Diaz describes his work as concerned with the relationship between technology, humanity, and what he calls our connection to the universe; he has specifically identified anatomy, computer science, mathematics, cosmology, biology, quantum physics, and consciousness among his areas of inquiry.

An authorized archival edition of *Cosmic Overture* measures **24 × 24.3 inches** and is printed on acid-free archival paper with aqueous archival inks. That dimension refers to **the print edition**, not necessarily the original drawing.
