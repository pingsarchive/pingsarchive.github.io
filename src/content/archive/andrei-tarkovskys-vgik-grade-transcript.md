**Attachment to Diploma No. N-756038.** The transcript records Andrei Tarkovsky’s studies in the Department of Film Directing at the All-Union State Institute of Cinematography (VGIK) between 1954 and 1960. His coursework included Marxist-Leninist aesthetics, literature, fine arts, music, Soviet and foreign cinema, camera skills, dramaturgy, acting, directing, editing, and photographic composition. Most subjects are recorded with the highest grade. His diploma work, the short feature *The Steamroller and the Violin*, also received the highest grade. The document is dated Moscow, 5 May 1961, Register No. 41. Item 21 of the transcript is inexplicably blank.

[Nostalghia.com](http://Nostalghia.com) states that its transcription was taken from *Андрей Тарковский: начало... и пути: воспоминания, интервью, лекции, статьи* (*Andrei Tarkovsky: Beginning... and Paths: Memoirs, Interviews, Lectures, Articles*), edited/compiled by M. A. Rostotskaya and published by VGIK in Moscow in 1994. The volume is 204 pages, ISBN 5-87149-014-X.
