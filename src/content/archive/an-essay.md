Originally written by Bianca Censori in response to an interview inquiry by POSTPOSTPOST.

The essay centers heavily on Catherine Malabou’s concept of plasticity — the capacity to give form, receive form, and destroy form — and extends that framework to identity, performance, public image, architecture, relationships, and womanhood.
