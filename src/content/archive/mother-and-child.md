archiveDescription: Selected sculptures from Han Meilin’s Mother and Child (母与子)
  series, a recurring body of work centered on maternal love and the physical
  relationship between mother and child. Across the series, the human figure is
  elongated, compressed, curved, and joined into highly simplified compositions
  in which arms, torsos, gesture, and negative space become the primary means of
  expression. Rather than repeating a single composition, Han continually
  reorganizes the mother and child around acts of holding, lifting, sheltering,
  reaching, and play.
reason: the mother and child stop functioning as two separate figures and become
  a single system of lines around one another. holding, carrying, lifting,
  reaching, and protecting become the structure of the sculpture itself.
