The sketch originated from the archives of designers David and Elizabeth Emanuel and was contained in a folder labeled “The 'Gulf' Tour 1986, Day & Evening Wear Designs.” RR Auction described it as one of five original hand-drawn pencil outfit designs prepared for Diana's 1986 Gulf tour. The outfit was conceived as a reserve option for Saudi Arabia and was never worn. Elizabeth Emanuel later stated that it was not included in the final group of designs presented to the Princess once the designers had a clearer understanding of the tour's dress requirements.
