The chandelier was not ordinarily installed in the slaughterhouse. According to the original AP caption, the manufacturer was located nearby but lacked enough interior space to assemble such a large fixture, so the slaughterhouse was temporarily used for the work. The final destination and identity of the Arab ruler who commissioned it were deliberately withheld, as was its price.
