The garment uses Maison Martin Margiela’s numeric label with 10 circled, identifying it as part of the house’s menswear collection. The “I DON’T SMOKE” typography is applied to the reverse/interior face of the shirt. Seen from the conventional exterior, the lettering reads backwards and appears faded through the fabric. Archival examples describe the piece as reversible.
