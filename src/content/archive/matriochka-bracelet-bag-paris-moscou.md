Chanel’s Matriochka bracelet bag was produced for the house’s **Paris–Moscou 2008/09 Métiers d’Art collection**, created under Karl Lagerfeld. Contemporary documentation reproducing Chanel’s original product information lists the piece as **“Matriochka (Russian doll) enamel bracelet bag,” reference A47525 Y06113 C0286**.

The bag translates the traditional Russian nesting doll into a miniature sculptural minaudière. Its glossy black body is interrupted by a metallic face, repeated interlocking CC emblems and jewel-like floral ornamentation, while a short chain functions as both handle and bracelet.

Sotheby’s catalogs a corresponding black Lucite example as part of Chanel’s limited-edition **Pre-Fall 2009 Métiers d’Art Paris–Moscou collection**, measuring approximately 18 cm high and 6 cm wide and deep.

The object belongs to Lagerfeld’s broader Paris–Moscou collection, which drew heavily on Russian decorative motifs and transformed them into Chanel accessories, jewelry and garments.
