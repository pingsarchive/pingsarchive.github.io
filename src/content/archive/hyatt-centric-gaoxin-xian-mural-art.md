The project began in December 2019 and was completed in December 2023. The mural is approximately 61 meters long and 5 meters high. The first section resembles an abstract dragon head and takes formal inspiration from an engine. The second section incorporates three horses derived from a Terracotta Army horse, a jade horse, and a stone-carved horse, corresponding to the Qin, Han, and Tang dynasties. Circular strokes draw from the cursive script of Tang-dynasty calligrapher Zhang Xu. The mural forms part of Hyatt Centric Gaoxin Xi’an, whose architecture was designed by Kengo Kuma. The project received a Golden A’ Design Award and was also recognized by the Golden Pin Design Award.
