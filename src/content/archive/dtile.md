archiveDescription: |-
  DTILE is a modular ceramic tile system developed by Dutch designers
  Arnout Visser, Erik Jan Kwakkel, and Peter van der Jagt to extend the
  logic of the tile grid across three-dimensional spaces and objects.

  Rather than allowing corners, fixtures, storage, or utilities to interrupt
  the tiled surface, the system incorporates curved construction tiles and
  functional tiles directly into the grid. Hooks, outlets, shelves, drawers,
  vents, drains, controls, sinks, and other household functions can become
  part of the tiled surface itself.
kind: modular ceramic tile system / industrial design / architectural surface system
reason: the idea of treating every interruption — a corner, shelf, hook, drain,
  drawer, outlet — as something that can be absorbed into one continuous visual
  system rather than added on afterward
