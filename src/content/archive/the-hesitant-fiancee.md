Also known as The Hesitant Betrothed and La Fiancée Hésitante. Sotheby’s records the painting as signed “A. Toulmouche” and dated 1866 at lower left. It may have been exhibited at the Paris Salon of 1866 as no. 185 and at the Exposition Universelle of 1867 as no. 591. The work later appeared at Christie’s, New York, on October 24, 1990, lot 122, before being offered at Sotheby’s. Toulmouche was known for genre paintings of fashionably dressed women in luxurious interiors; The Hesitant Fiancée is unusual for the psychological tension created among its four female figures.
