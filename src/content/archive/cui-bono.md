*Cui bono* measures 71 × 91 cm. Marion Jones painted it in Chelsea, London, and inscribed her name in black paint at the lower left. The artist presented the work to the National Gallery of Victoria under the terms of the National Gallery of Victoria Travelling Scholarship in 1924. It is held in the Australian Painting department under accession number 1295-3 and is not currently on display.
