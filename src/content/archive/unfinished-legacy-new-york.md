Unfinished Legacy was founded by Sudanese-born artist and designer Brema Brema. Butterflies recur throughout the brand's work; Brema has discussed them as symbols connected to growth, migration and transformation. The apple functions as an immediately recognizable New York symbol while the graffiti treatment connects it to the street-art origins of Brema's practice. The bottom caption identifies the image explicitly as “UNFINISHED LEGACY NEW YORK.”
