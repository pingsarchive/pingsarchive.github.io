Published as Figure 7 on report page 8 of *Traversable Wormholes, Stargates, and Negative Energy*, DIA-08-1004-004, dated 6 April 2010. The figure is captioned “A Stargate (adapted from Reference 9).” Reference 9 is Michio Kaku, *Hyperspace: A Scientific Odyssey Through Parallel Universes, Time-Warps, and the 10th Dimension* (1994; Anchor edition 1995), illustrated by Robert O’Keefe. The dinosaur and disembodied-arm scenarios correspond to Kaku’s Figures 10.3 and 10.4.
