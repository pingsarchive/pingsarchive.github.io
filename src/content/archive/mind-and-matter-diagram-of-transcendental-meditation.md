Published in the New Statesman alongside David Lynch's essay “Heaven is a place on earth” on 31 October 2013, where it was captioned “Mind and matter: Lynch's diagram of Transcendental Meditation” and credited © David Lynch. Lynch used the diagram to explain his conception of mind and matter, mantra, transcending, pure consciousness, the Unified Field, Atma, Brahm, Tao, and the relationship between expanding consciousness and the reduction of negativity. A later account suggests the published diagram may derive from a drawing Lynch made while explaining Transcendental Meditation during material connected with the documentary Meditation Creativity Peace.
