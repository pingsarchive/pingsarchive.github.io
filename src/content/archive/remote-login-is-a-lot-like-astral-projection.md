This illustration appears as Figure 7.1 in Chapter 7, “Remote-Login with Telnet,” of Daniel P. Dern’s *The Internet Guide for New Users*. The section introducing remote login is titled “Astral Projection for Computers,” and the figure caption reads, “Remote login is a lot like astral projection.” The book was published by McGraw-Hill in 1994 and was illustrated by Hannah M. G. Shapero.

The image is notable for translating an early-networking concept into an explicitly metaphysical visual metaphor: the computer user’s body remains at one terminal while a projected version of the self appears at a distant machine. Although the illustration now circulates online largely detached from its original context, it began as explanatory imagery for people learning how to use the early internet.
