archiveDescription: A hollow hand-grenade sculpture constructed from Chinese
  yuan banknotes, with Mao Zedong’s portrait wrapping across the body of the
  weapon. Part of Justine Smith’s Instruments of State series, the work brings
  together currency, political imagery and military form, using the physical
  fragility of paper money to complicate the apparent solidity of economic and
  state power.
reason: the transformation of an ordinary banknote into a sculptural weapon; the
  way Mao’s portrait and the existing architecture of the currency become part
  of the object rather than simply decorating it.
