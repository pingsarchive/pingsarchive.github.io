Scott Catalogue no. 1371. Face value: 6 cents. Issued May 5, 1969. Designed by Leonard E. Buckley and printed by the Bureau of Engraving and Printing. The design incorporates NASA photograph 68-HC-870, commonly known as *Earthrise*. The image was made during Apollo 8’s December 1968 lunar mission. The inscription “In the beginning God…” references the crew’s Christmas Eve reading from Genesis. Approximately 187,165,000 stamps were issued.
