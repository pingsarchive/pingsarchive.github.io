The Corner Houses series concentrates on mixed residential-commercial buildings dating largely from Hong Kong’s postwar decades. Buchkunst Berlin describes the structures as products of the pragmatism and economic constraints of the 1950s and 1960s and presents them as overlooked elements of Hong Kong’s urban development. The gallery page reproduces works including Corner Houses #22, #29, #13, #3, #20, #32, #33, #7, #47, #8, #15, and #42. Buchkunst Berlin lists editions in three paper sizes: 50 × 40 cm (edition of 9 + 2 AP), 86 × 68 cm (edition of 7 + 2 AP), and 150 × 120 cm (edition of 3 + 2 AP). The series was exhibited in Hong Kong by 2010, and the photobook Hong Kong Corner Houses was published by Hong Kong University Press in 2011.
